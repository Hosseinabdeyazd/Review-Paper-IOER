PRISMA accounting and reproducibility

The selected set has 21 reports sought, one not retrieved, 20 assessed, zero full-text exclusions and 20 included. It is separate from the broad search denominators. Missing links between broad screening and focused selection cannot be reconstructed by subtraction. All source hashes, record-level memberships, category decisions and full-text context reasons accompany this report in CSV files. No unassessed record is labelled as lacking uncertainty analysis.

Population-wide uncertainty-treatment rates for S1–S5 remain unestablished. The reported rates are exact arithmetic for the specified archived cohorts, with the S1 provenance qualification retained. Wider substantive rates require systematic content review of the remaining records, including Base-only records.
