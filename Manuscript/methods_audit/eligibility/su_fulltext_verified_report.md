## Verification method

The source documents were inspected at the methods, relevant results and limitation passages recorded in the CSV. PDF page numbers refer to file pages, with printed pages supplied where useful. Publisher-text sources use section/figure locators. This is targeted full-text eligibility and mechanism verification, not a claim that every paragraph of every document or supplement was read. File hashes and reviewed versions identify the actual evidence. Earlier extraction labels alone were not accepted as evidence. Supplemental PDFs were not re-assessed in this pass.

## Consequences for synthesis

U11 and U12 directly support quantitative stock–flow propagation. U18 shows how survival conditioning and layer-specific lifetimes transform renovation flows. U13 supports a connected inventory–flows–impact workflow while explicitly leaving output uncertainty quantification incomplete. U19 directly supports a reuse-quality/CFF environmental-impact interface, but does not justify assuming an entirely specified joint uncertainty model. U03 and U04 support input inference and probabilistic preparation rather than complete end-to-end propagation; U07 and U09 show heterogeneity/design variability, which must not be mislabeled probabilistic uncertainty. U15 must not be counted as direct S2→S3 evidence. U20 and U21 provide complementary hazard/loss-to-impact mechanisms, not direct material-recovery evidence. U02 and U14 are secondary sources and must be distinguished from primary studies in evidence totals.

The selected set does not establish continuous uncertainty propagation across all S1–S5 boundaries. In particular, available direct evidence for the outflow-to-actually-recoverable-material boundary remains limited. Absence of demonstrated joint propagation in the reviewed passages is an evidence limitation, not proof that the mechanism cannot exist.
