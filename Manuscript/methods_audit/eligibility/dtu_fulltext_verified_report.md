# Focused DT + uncertainty: full-text eligibility reassessment

Scope: the existing 18-report focused DT-U set, not the broad S1-S5 + DT + U retrieval stream. Original local PDFs were extracted page by page and substantive methods, results and limitations sections were inspected. The CSV records exactly which PDF page ranges supplied this reassessment. This is targeted full-text assessment, not a claim that every word of every report was reread.

## Decisions

- Reports sought: 18.
- Complete reports available and assessed: 13.
- Reports not retrieved: 5 (DTUM-003, 005, 006, 007, 017).
- Reports included in differentiated evidence synthesis: 13.
- Substantive full-text exclusions among the assessed set: 0.

Zero exclusions follows the broad, transferable-mechanism remit and explicit retention of secondary and complementary architecture evidence. It does **not** mean 13 validated DT implementations. Five reports provide primary mechanism evidence (002, 012, 013, 016, 018); three are transferable inference/UQ/decision components (004, 014, 015); three are secondary methodological/metrological/architecture syntheses (001, 009, 010); two are complementary/conceptual architecture reports (008, 011). The last two must not be counted as demonstrations of calibrated uncertainty propagation.

The strongest direct interface evidence is posterior/particle updating in 002, conditional state/parameter inference in 004, covariance-to-action adaptation in 016, and state/filter versus downstream-prediction interfaces in 018. Report 018 specifically does not demonstrate that the filter covariance survives into the sequence-model input. Reports 013 and 014 use distributional representations but do not establish an end-to-end joint uncertainty ledger. None establishes material-flow S1-S5 continuity by itself.

DTUM-004 was read as the available arXiv preprint, not an assumed identical publisher version. DTUM-006 DOI and ScienceDirect recovery attempts failed; partial indexed passages are not sufficient to mark its complete text retrieved. No access failure is coded as a scientific exclusion.

Possible related-report pairs 010/012 and 013/014 are flagged. These are report counts; independent underlying-study counts have not been inferred from different DOIs. No claim is made that the broad corpus or all manuscript PRISMA streams are complete.

PDFs remain in their original local folder. File hashes in the CSV identify the reviewed versions. Decisions were made with AI assistance and should not be described as independent duplicate human screening.
