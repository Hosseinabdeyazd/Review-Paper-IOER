# Broad Base + DT + U: six existing full texts

All six locally available matched reports were reassessed against the triple-intersection scope, using original methods/results/limitations text. Two are included as primary research reports with **conceptual**, not demonstrated deployed, DT status. Four are excluded from this strict mechanism intersection but remain relevant to other evidence streams. There are no secondary-review inclusions among these six.

Included: BIM–COSMOS conceptual stochastic control (10.3390/buildings16153012) and modular-timber SCDT survey/simulation (10.30564/jbms.v8i1.12686). Neither establishes complete uncertainty continuity across S1-S5 or a validated autonomous DT. The timber study particularly needs cautious weighting because its DT variable is principally a maturity construct and implementation detail is limited.

Excluded: nested archetyping, BIM–GIS stock assessment and Egyptian wall-carbon substitution lack explicit uncertainty analysis beyond fixed-input comparisons, correction, or limitations. Probabilistic subsoil excavation does propagate GPR uncertainty into volumes, duration, cost and emissions, but DT is contextual rather than an implemented or specifically operationalized architecture in that report. It is valuable S+U evidence and must not be discarded from the overall review.

Full end-to-end propagation is **not** an inclusion requirement: substantive uncertainty/error analysis plus a substantive DT role is sufficient, and the degree of continuity is extracted as a finding. Accuracy-validation-only (for example a single geometric correction factor or a BOQ deviation check) is kept distinct from uncertainty analysis. Qualitative error-source caveats without an analysis do not meet this criterion.

Conceptual DT is eligible when its operational roles/data linkage or study construct are substantive. Generic DT motivation is not. Scenario alternatives are not automatically uncertainty realizations. This criterion prevents labelling all retrieved papers as positive evidence merely because their metadata matched the query. Inclusion denotes primary research evidence, not validation quality, and not direct proof of an uncertainty-tracking software architecture.

These decisions apply only to the six locally available publications. They do not complete full-text eligibility for the broad528 corpus. CSV preserves exact locations, evidence, hashes and limitations for audit.
