# ID06 — Nie et al. 2025 main-text recovery and transition review
DOI: 10.1021/acs.est.5c03058. Retrieved 2026-11-02 via SLUB institutional subscription to ACS. Main PDF14pages plus34pageSI stored. This replaces the earlier SI-only evidence status. No Digital Twin eligibility criterion is applied.

## Stage mapping
S1→S2: satellite footprints, height and building typology determine floor area and material inventory. S2→S3: construction/demolition dates and stock differences determine material inflows/outflows. S2/S3→S5: material quantities and production/disposal emission factors determine embodied emissions; cement masses, age/exposure and carbonation parameters determine uptake. No modeled recoverability/reuse-yield stage S4 is established.

## What is propagated or transformed
- Main pp4–5, section2.3: national annual material-intensity means are combined with relative P25/P75 deviations from the RASMI median. Material stock=sum footprint×height/3m×MI. This rescales MI ranges into time-specific quantity estimates. Range construction is explicit, but joint stochastic propagation of footprint/height/classification errors is not demonstrated.
- Main p6, section2.4.1: 1000 Monte Carlo draws of technology market-share ranges and technology-specific emission-factor ranges yield period-specific weighted emission-factor distributions, summarized asP25/P50/P75. Multiplication by material quantities transforms uncertainty in production technology/emission intensity into embodied-carbon uncertainty.
- Disposal(C3–C4) factors assume the same temporal trend and relative uncertainty as production; this is an imposed linkage, not measured recovery-performance evidence. Transport uses fixed factor and average distances; on-site stages empirical floor-number equations.
- Main p6 and SI ppS3–S4/TableS9: cement carbonation depends on material mass, clinker/CaO fractions, degree of carbonation and diffusion/exposure parameters. These introduce physical-process uncertainty beyond material quantities.
- Main p10, section3.4: annual emissionsCV averages10.86%; carbonationCV38.8%; reported P10/P90 deviations differ. These compare different output quantities; they do NOT measure an S1→S5 amplification factor for a shared input uncertainty.

## Where continuity is not demonstrated
Height stability across25randomseeds (mainp3, medianpixelCV<0.025), footprintF1, typology validation, construction/demolition-yearF1 are local accuracy assessments. The article does not show that their errors are jointly sampled and carried into final stocks/flows/carbon. Annual building probabilities are reduced to selected event years by temporal segmentation. Dependence/covariance across buildings, material intensities, years and emission factors, and sample identity across modules are not specified.

## Revised classification
Full main text+SI verified. Mixed/partial propagation: explicit downstream factor-distribution construction and uncertain environmental outputs; upstream prediction-error continuity remains unverified. Keep as a useful multistage quantity→impact chain, not as proof of complete uncertainty continuity or of S3→S4→S5 recovery propagation.
