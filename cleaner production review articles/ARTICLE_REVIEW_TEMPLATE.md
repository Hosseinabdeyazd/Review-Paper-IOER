## 5. Evidence ledger

Assign an ID such as E01 to each important point.

### E01 — Point title

**Source evidence:** Concise and accurate paraphrase with DOI, section, page, and, where necessary, table/figure number.

**Claim boundary:** Is this an empirical result, a literature-review finding, a conceptual example, or an author recommendation? What scope and time period does it apply to?

**Our interpretation:** What role does this point play in the logic of the article?

**Proposed adaptation:** What change might be useful for our project? Do not present it as a finding of the reviewed article.

**Action ID:** Complete after the proposal is entered into the action register.

## 9. What should not be copied or generalized?

Record limitations of the reported method, topical scope, terminology differences, evidence limitations, context-dependent arguments, and time-sensitive novelty claims. A lack of evidence in one article does not establish a lack of evidence in the current literature as a whole.
