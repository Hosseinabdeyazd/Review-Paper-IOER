# Selected papers S1–S5 + Uncertainty

This collection contains **21 distinct DOI records**: 18 retained candidates from the earlier 19-paper S1–S3 pilot audit and three subsequently reviewed S4–S5 papers. The excluded operational-energy review (10.1186/s42162-024-00313-7) is not included. Digital Twin is not an inclusion criterion.

The 21 records are a **working evidence set**, not 21 proven interstage-propagation studies and not a completed PRISMA inclusion set. Two S4–S5 additions are complementary damage-to-carbon mechanisms rather than direct uncertain-recovery studies. The earlier shorthand “19 core + 2 complementary” was a bookkeeping description, not an independently validated eligibility classification.

## Files and evidence

- `selected_papers.csv`: all 21 DOI records, original and audited transition labels, methods, evidence limitations and local fulltext status.
- `transition_extraction.csv`: focused interface-level extraction, with one paper per row and multiple transitions where appropriate.
- `papers/`: locally recovered main texts. A PDF and publisher text are distinguished explicitly. Missing texts remain listed; absence is never replaced by a dummy PDF.
- `file_manifest.json`: local file sizes and SHA256 hashes.
- `sources/`: preserved earlier extraction tables, including the excluded record for auditability.
- `REPORT_FA.md`: synthesis, gaps and interpretation in Persian.

The prior S1–S3 coding is retained with its original evidence status. “Full text verified” in the prior table describes that earlier audit, not a claim that every paper has just been reread. New downloads and newly reviewed evidence are recorded separately. Legacy S2→S3 labels on emissions-focused papers are not treated as demonstrated stock-to-demolition transitions: see `audited_transition`.

## Scope of stages

S1: acquisition/inference of building attributes. S2: material intensity, composition and stock estimation. S3: temporal stock dynamics, lifetime, renovation and demolition/material outflows. S4: recovery, reuse and circular routing/quality. S5: environmental consequences and assessment. These functional labels belong to the material-flow review and must not be confused with a separate DT design/configuration figure.

## Reproduction

The collection builder validates 21 unique DOI records, preserves prior extraction and copies verified downloads into stable U01–U21 filenames. Download manifests retain source URLs. The GitHub collection publishes the extraction, reports and reproducibility inputs. Fulltext availability and public redistribution rights are separate: institutional downloads are retained locally unless redistribution is established.

Date: 2026-11-02. AI-assisted synthesis of recorded evidence; no claim of independent human double screening or rerunning authors' models.
