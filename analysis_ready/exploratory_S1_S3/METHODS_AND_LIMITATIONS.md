# Analysis protocol

The exploratory analysis addresses five questions:

1. How rapidly are S1-S3 evidence bases growing?
2. How does uncertainty-oriented retrieval change over time relative to the broad corpus?
3. Which methodological signals characterize each stage and how do they evolve?
4. How often do records appear across adjacent stages, indicating candidate interfaces?
5. Are these patterns sensitive to records flagged by the noise diagnostic?

The analysis preserves the review's distinction between uncertainty-oriented retrieval and verified uncertainty treatment. Sensitivity analysis, robustness, classifier performance, or probabilistic terminology do not constitute uncertainty propagation unless downstream transfer is demonstrated in full text.

Cross-stage overlap is therefore reported as a candidate bridge only.
