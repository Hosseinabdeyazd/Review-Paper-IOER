# Exploratory S1-S3 analysis — noise-aware

## Status

This analysis uses the immutable current_merged snapshots from the noise/precision diagnostic. It is exploratory and pre-screening.

Candidate-noise flags are used only for sensitivity analysis; no paper is automatically excluded. U/B ratios use only uncertainty-layer records that are also present in the corresponding broad layer, preserving the intended U subset B logic.

The 2027-dated S1 record is excluded from temporal-period calculations because the search date is 2026. The 2026 publication year is incomplete and should not be compared as a full calendar year.

## Main signals

- S1 shows a strong temporal shift from remote-sensing framing toward ML/DL framing.
- S2 remains centered on material-stock quantification but shows growing BIM/digital-building terminology in recent years.
- S3 combines dynamic MFA, lifetime/demolition, and scenario forecasting; survival/hazard language remains a smaller niche in the broad corpus.
- S1 uncertainty-attention share rises across periods, while S2 and S3 do not show a monotonic trend.
- Candidate cross-stage papers are rare relative to the size of stage-specific corpora, especially across all three stages.
- Candidate-noise flags affect very few bridge papers, so the cross-stage fragmentation signal is not explained by the diagnostic noise flags.
- U-layer terminology must not be interpreted as verified uncertainty propagation. Full-text coding remains required.

## Interpretation boundary

Method and uncertainty signals are based on transparent keyword dictionaries applied to title, abstract, and keywords. Categories overlap and represent textual evidence signals rather than final manual codes.
