# Preliminary full-text uncertainty results — S1-S3 transition audit

## Status

This folder contains a provisional full-text/SI audit of the current 19-paper uncertainty-transition candidate set.

- 17 papers have sufficiently verified full-text or supporting-information evidence for the current statistical summaries.
- 1 paper remains provisional because only abstract-level evidence is available in the supplied archive.
- 1 operational-energy-focused Digital Twin review was screened out of the core material-stock/circularity transition chain.

These results are **not final systematic-review results**. They are a pilot analytical layer that will be updated after formal screening and full-text eligibility of the complete evidence base.

## Three-axis coding architecture

Each paper is coded separately for:

1. **Uncertainty source/type** — where uncertainty originates (U01-U13).
2. **Uncertainty method** — how uncertainty is represented or analysed (UM00-UM10).
3. **Propagation outcome** — what happens to uncertainty at a stage interface: Preserved, Legitimately reduced, Amplified, Transformed, Masked/lost, or Unassessed.

Source/type and method are multi-label. Propagation outcome is coded at the transition level where the evidence allows it.

## Preliminary signals

- Material intensity/composition uncertainty (U04) is the dominant source signal: 11/17 papers (64.7%).
- Scenario/future-context uncertainty (U12) appears in 7/17 (41.2%), and temporal/event uncertainty (U06) in 6/17 (35.3%).
- One-way/local sensitivity analysis is the most frequent uncertainty method: 6/17 (35.3%).
- More than half of the audited papers leave the downstream fate of uncertainty unassessed: 9/17 (52.9%).
- Transformation is observed in 7/17 (41.2%); explicit preservation in 3/17 (17.6%).
- The interface contrast is large: at least partial propagation is observed in 1/9 S1→S2 papers (11.1%) versus 6/9 S2→S3 papers (66.7%).

The interface comparison is the strongest current pilot signal: uncertainty handling appears substantially more mature at the material-stock → stock-dynamics interface than at the GeoAI → material-stock interface.
