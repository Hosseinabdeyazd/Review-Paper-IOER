# Provisional title/abstract screening pilot — current S1-S3 multi-stage records

This pilot screens the 73 records that currently occur in at least two Broad S1-S3 corpora.

It is **not the final PRISMA screening of the full 1,212-record S1-S3 union**. Its purpose is to test whether restricting the early transition audit to U-layer intersections missed relevant deterministic or non-U bridge papers.

## Screening logic

- Include: title/abstract explicitly links at least two relevant analytical stages or outputs.
- Unclear: relevant, but the cross-stage link cannot be confirmed from title/abstract alone; retain for full-text eligibility.
- Secondary evidence: review/chapter relevant to the topic but not treated as primary empirical transition evidence; retain for background and snowballing.
- Exclude: clearly outside the material-stock/circularity evidence chain under the current criteria.

U-status is not an inclusion criterion.

## Current pilot result

- Clear Include: 62
- Unclear: 5
- Primary records proceeding to full-text eligibility: 67
- Secondary evidence: 5
- Exclude: 1

The earlier 19-paper uncertainty-transition set is therefore a **nested uncertainty-focused subset**, not the complete cross-stage evidence base.

All decisions remain provisional until full formal screening and S4-S5 retrieval are completed.
