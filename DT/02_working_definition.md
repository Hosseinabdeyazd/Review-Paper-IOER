# Our provisional operational definition — a falsifiable hypothesis

**Status: PROPOSED v0.1; NOT a discovered or adopted consensus definition.**

> **Working formulation (our synthesis, not an attributable quotation):** A circular built-environment digital twin is a purpose-bounded digital representation of identifiable real-world building assets, components, materials and material-related processes, maintained through a documented mechanism for incorporating observations and physical events at decision-appropriate intervals. The system connects persistent entity identities and temporal states with analytical services for material stocks, flows, circular pathways and environmental consequences, and makes its outputs usable in traceable human or automated decisions.

## Why this wording is provisional

1. **Minimum generic DT / domain-specific functions:** physical referent, digital representation and ongoing relation to observations are candidates for the minimum; MFA, LCA, circularity and uncertainty are *domain/application functions*, not universal admission criteria.
2. **Not necessarily real-time:** a multi-decade material-stock application may update upon renovation, audit, demolition or verified material transaction; the appropriate cadence must be recorded and empirically defended. Abdelrahman et al. (2025), PDF pp. 13–15, and Geremicca et al. (2026), PDF p. 10/§2.2 support purpose-dependent time scales.
3. **Feedback is not always actuation:** receiving field records, model recalibration, stakeholder decision and physical intervention are distinct directed relations. Human-mediated action ≠ automated control. Study each separately.
4. **Do not bake novelty into admission criteria:** uncertainty continuity and provenance completeness are evaluated as *quality dimensions*, not required simply to qualify as a DT. Otherwise the review becomes circular.
5. **A digital city is not a single giant BIM:** multiple building stocks, infrastructure systems, resource markets, policy processes and actors may be represented through federated or hierarchical interconnections; such structures need explicit evidence, not a label.

## Boundary-testing cases (to be adjudicated by the review)

| Candidate system | Initial working classification | What evidence could change the classification? |
|---|---|---|
| Static BIM with component-material attributes | Digital model, not yet proven DT | Documented linkage to observed physical changes with updates |
| GIS + dynamic MFA + LCA, with no documented update from reality | Integrated analytical platform / prospective DT | Evidence of state synchronization to specified actual buildings and events |
| Periodically updated cadastral building stocks with stable asset IDs | Dynamic registry; DT status unresolved | How it connects representation, processing, validation and decisions to actual changes |
| BIM/BAS plus environmental models, event-based updates and human action | Potential building DT | Inspect documented observation, update and feedback paths; verify vs conceptual diagram |
| Building-component datasets queried by a city-wide material twin | Potential component-to-city federation | Identity reconciliation, access rights, multi-scale aggregation, update ownership and synchronization |
| Scenario engine with no physical referent | Scenario simulation, not necessarily DT | Establish and validate a physical entity/process and synchronisation mechanism |

## Independent quality axes (do not conflate)

- **Physical–digital state fidelity:** do the current represented values match available observations at their required spatial/temporal resolution?
- **Inter-model information continuity:** can outputs and semantics be traced from source model to downstream model?
- **Inter-model uncertainty continuity:** are uncertainties, dependencies and assumptions represented and carried through transformations?
- **Decision validity:** do output intervals/alternatives meet a decision's tolerance, and has this been validated?

The distinction between the first two loops is a *testable analytic proposal*. Generic DT definitions and documented technology capacities are **literature-derived**; the cross-model uncertainty criterion is a **proposed systematic-review evaluation lens**.
