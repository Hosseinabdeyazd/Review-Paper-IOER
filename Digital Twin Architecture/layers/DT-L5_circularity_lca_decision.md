# DT-L5 — Circularity, LCA & Decision Intelligence

## Status
Working scaffold — not yet analyzed in detail.

## Core question
How should released material stocks be converted into circularity and environmental decision scenarios under explicit uncertainty?

## Candidate decision variables
- material quantity
- material quality
- contamination
- technical recoverability
- actual recovery
- reuse suitability
- recycling suitability
- open-loop vs closed-loop
- substitution ratio
- replacement coefficient
- market availability
- transport distance
- processing requirements
- future demand
- end-of-life scenario
- LCA consequences

## Candidate outputs
- scenario-specific material flows
- reuse / recycling / recovery potential
- effective virgin-material substitution
- avoided impacts
- residual disposal
- uncertainty intervals / distributions
- decision robustness

## Feedback role
If decision uncertainty is too high, DT-L5 should be able to request improved evidence from DT-L1–L4.

## Uncertainty analysis
Focus on quality loss, contamination, recovery rates, market conditions, substitution assumptions, EoL choices, LCA model choices and decision robustness.
