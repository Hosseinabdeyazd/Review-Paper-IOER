IEEE Xplore S3 search execution notes
Search date: 2026-09-22

Scope and retrieval rule
- Database: IEEE Xplore Advanced Search -> Command Search.
- Every Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY term was translated to an IEEE Xplore "All Metadata" term. No year, language, Open Access, publication-type, subject-area, or other filters were applied.
- The Scopus AND NOT exclusion block was intentionally omitted from every IEEE retrieval. Exclusions are reserved for later post-processing on Title/Abstract/Keywords.
- The raw CSV exports were not merged or deduplicated. Overlap between files is therefore retained.
- IEEE exported 28 bibliographic fields, including title, authors, affiliations, publication title, abstract, DOI, author keywords, IEEE terms, publication year, document identifier, and document type/publisher information.

S3-1 wildcard-limit adjustment
- Original S3-1 block B contains 11 wildcard operators, exceeding IEEE Xplore's maximum of 10 per query.
- B was split without changing its Boolean meaning. The common building-stock group was retained in both parts, while the second OR group was partitioned as follows:
  - B-part1: building lifetime*, building lifespan*, service life distribution*, lifetime distribution*, demolition rate*, demolition probabilit*.
  - B-part2: demolition timing, demolition record*, demolition data.
- The nonzero B-part1 export is named IEEE_S3-1B_raw.csv because B-part2 returned 0 records; no B records were merged or deduplicated.

S3-2 uncertainty partitions
The original uncertainty block was preserved as a union of disjoint term partitions. Each base query was combined with every required partition for that base; this is logically equivalent to base AND (U1 OR U2 ...), while respecting IEEE's wildcard limit.

- U_A1: uncertaint*, uncertain*, probabil*, stochastic*, Bayesian, "Monte Carlo", sensitiv*, variabil* (6 wildcards).
- U_A2: robust*, calibrat*, "prediction interval*", "confidence interval*", "credible interval*", bootstrap* (6 wildcards).
- U_A3: ensemble*, "scenario uncertainty", "parameter uncertainty", "model uncertainty", "value of information" (1 wildcard).
- U_E1: uncertaint*, uncertain*, probabil*, stochastic*, Bayesian, "Monte Carlo" (4 wildcards).
- U_E2: sensitiv*, variabil*, robust*, calibrat* (4 wildcards).
- U_E3: "prediction interval*", "confidence interval*", "credible interval*", bootstrap* (4 wildcards).
- U_E4: ensemble*, "scenario uncertainty", "parameter uncertainty", "model uncertainty", "value of information" (1 wildcard).
- U_D1: uncertaint*, uncertain*, probabil*, stochastic*, Bayesian, "Monte Carlo", sensitiv*, variabil*, robust* (7 wildcards).
- U_D2: calibrat*, "prediction interval*", "confidence interval*", "credible interval*", bootstrap*, ensemble*, "scenario uncertainty", "parameter uncertainty", "model uncertainty", "value of information" (6 wildcards).

S3-2 block B adjustment
- The S3-2 B base was partitioned into four disjoint life-cycle OR subsets, each retaining the common four-term building-stock OR group:
  - L1: building lifetime* OR building lifespan*.
  - L2: service life distribution* OR lifetime distribution*.
  - L3: demolition rate* OR demolition probabilit*.
  - L4: demolition timing OR demolition record* OR demolition data.
- Each L1-L4 subset was combined with U_E1-U_E4. This preserves the original B AND uncertainty logic without exceeding the wildcard cap.

Results summary
- S3-1 nonzero blocks: A = 2; B = 1; F = 1. C, D, E, G, and the second B split returned 0.
- S3-2 nonzero partitions: A + U_A1 = 2; A + U_A2 = 1. All other S3-2 partitions returned 0.
- The S3-2 A-U1 export overlaps S3-1A by design; no deduplication was applied.

Errors and authentication
- No IEEE query returned a syntax error or other failed-query message.
- IEEE personal authentication was completed successfully through the secure browser sign-in flow; the authenticated page displayed "Welcome Hossein Abdeyazdan" and "Sign Out" before exports.
